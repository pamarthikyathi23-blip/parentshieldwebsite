Keep your child's phone under your control

ParentShield is an Android app for parents. You choose which apps are blocked on a child's phone. When a blocked app is opened, a lock screen appears, and only a parent can open it, with a fingerprint, face or voice.

Works on Android 7.0 and newer. The app is shared directly and is not on the Play Store yet. Write to us to get it.

What the app does

  • Block apps. Pick the apps you want locked, or allow only a short list of safe apps.
  • Parent unlock. A blocked app opens only after the parent proves it is them: fingerprint or phone face unlock, or ParentShield's own Face ID and Voice ID.
  • Separate profiles. Each child or adult gets their own rules. Face ID or Voice ID switches to the right person.
  • School Hours. Apps you allow for school time open on their own during the hours you set, and holidays are skipped.
  • Parent's Office. Apps open on their own when the phone is at the place you choose, such as your office.
  • Your own lock-screen message, written or spoken, so the child hears it from you.
  • Handover. One switch to lock every restricted app before you hand over the phone.
