Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

ParentShield is run by Kyathi Ramadevi Pamarthi ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the ParentShield app and our server keep, why, and what stays only on your phone.

What stays on your phone and is never sent to us

  • The list of apps you block or allow, for each profile.
  • School Hours, holidays, and the location you set as Parent's Office.
  • Face ID: when you scan a face, the photo is used once to make a short set of numbers and is then deleted. Only those numbers are kept, on the phone. The numbers cannot be turned back into a photo. The face check runs on the phone.
  • Voice ID and voice warnings: recordings are saved in ParentShield's private storage on the phone.
  • Profile names and your lock-screen messages.

If you uninstall ParentShield, this information is removed from the phone.

What our server keeps

Our server is used only for sign-in and plans. It keeps:

  • Your mobile number, to sign you in and to give the free first month once per number.
  • Sign-in codes, for a few minutes only, stored in scrambled form, and deleted once used or expired.
  • Plan and payment records: which plan you bought, the dates it runs, the amount, the Razorpay order and payment numbers, and whether the payment succeeded.

We do not see or store your card number, UPI ID or bank details. Payments are handled by Razorpay on their own payment screen.

Who else handles information

  • Razorpay takes payments. Their handling of payment details is covered by their own privacy policy.
  • Our SMS provider delivers the sign-in code to your mobile number.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server that holds the records above.

We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising.

Children

ParentShield is used by a parent or guardian on a child's phone. It does not ask a child to create an account or give us any information. Everything about the child's phone use stays on that phone, as listed above.

How long we keep things, and deleting your information

We keep plan and payment records for as long as the law requires for accounts and taxes. You can ask us to delete your mobile number and sign-in details by writing to pamarthikyathi6@gmail.com. We may have to keep payment records we are legally required to keep.

Security

The app talks to our server over an encrypted connection. Payments are checked on our server against Razorpay's signature, so a payment cannot be faked from the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the information we hold to the minimum above.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
