Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Cancelling

ParentShield plans are paid once for 30 days or 365 days and do not renew automatically. There is nothing to cancel. If you do not want a plan after it ends, simply do not buy again. Your plan stays active until its end date.

When you can get a refund

  • You were charged but the plan did not start. If a payment was taken and your plan is not active within 24 hours, we will refund the full amount or start the plan, whichever you prefer.
  • You paid twice for the same thing by mistake. We will refund the extra payment in full.
  • Changed your mind. Ask within 7 days of your first purchase and we will refund the full amount. This is available once per mobile number.

After 7 days, a plan that is working is not refunded. When you move to a higher plan, you already receive credit for the unused days of your current plan, so that is not refunded separately.

How to ask

Email pamarthikyathi6@gmail.com from, or mentioning, the mobile number you signed in with, and include the date and amount of the payment (the Razorpay payment number helps if you have it). We reply within 2 working days.

How refunds are paid

Once we approve a refund, it is sent back to the same UPI account, card or bank account you paid from, through Razorpay. It usually reaches you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.
